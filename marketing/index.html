Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Is ClientStream accounting software?

No. ClientStream helps manage clients, quotes, invoices, payments, expenses and basic reports, but it is not a replacement for professional accounting, bookkeeping, tax or legal advice.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. ClientStream is designed to work across modern phones, tablets and desktop browsers so you can update a client on site and complete administration later.

Does Smart Write send messages automatically?

No. Smart Write helps draft customer communication, but you remain in control. Review and approve every message before it is sent or copied into your preferred communication app.

Does ClientStream support GST?

Yes. The application is designed around Australian defaults, including AUD, DD/MM/YYYY dates, GST terminology and the Australian financial year.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your existing records are preserved. Your account returns to the Free plan limits, and you can continue viewing and exporting your information. You may need to upgrade again before creating new records beyond those limits.

Can I export my records?

ClientStream is designed to provide practical exports for business records and EOFY preparation. Export availability can vary by plan and app version.

Is SMS included in the subscription?

ClientStream can prepare SMS copy and open your device’s messaging tools. Third-party carrier or messaging charges are separate unless the app clearly states otherwise.
