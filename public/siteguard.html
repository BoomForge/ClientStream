A practical setup that takes little attention

Choose the pages you actually control

Start with your homepage, contact page and one booking or service page. Only monitor sites that you own or are authorised to check.

Know the difference between an incident and a recovery

Incident emails report a detected problem; recovery emails report when the page starts responding again. A single monitoring location can also experience connectivity problems, so investigate important alerts using another source when practical.

Understand what an HTTP check cannot prove

A successful response does not mean an online booking completed, a shopping cart charged correctly or a dynamic form accepted submissions. Those require separate end-to-end testing.

Keep a sensible response plan

  • Check whether the alert is reproducible from another network.
  • Confirm hosting, DNS and SSL status before making changes.
  • Contact your hosting provider when a wider outage appears likely.
  • Use recovery notifications to confirm the page responds again.
